Data Deletion

passmgr does not require a developer-hosted account. You control deletion on the device and at the storage providers you configured.

Delete individual items

Swipe an item left and confirm Delete. This removes its local metadata and associated Keychain secret and records a deletion for synchronization.

Reset local data

Open Settings → Data Reset → Reset Local Data. Authenticate with the configured app lock and confirm. Remote backups are retained.

Reset all data

Open Settings → Data Reset → Reset All Data. Authenticate and confirm. passmgr attempts to remove configured remote objects, then removes local vault data, provider credentials, recovery material, and settings. Offline or unavailable providers may require manual deletion in that provider.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling removes the app container but is not a reliable substitute for Reset All Data: Apple Keychain or iCloud items and remote-provider objects may survive. Use the in-app reset first when possible.

Support email

To delete an email you sent to support, contact sxlfhmsl@hotmail.com with enough non-secret context to identify it.
