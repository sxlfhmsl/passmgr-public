Support

Start with the in-app manual or the public manual. For unresolved problems, email sxlfhmsl@hotmail.com.

Before contacting support

  1. Record the passmgr version/build from About.
  2. Describe the exact action and result.
  3. State the iPhone and iOS version.
  4. For AutoFill, confirm passmgr is enabled under iOS Password Options.
  5. For backup, use Test Connection and include only the provider type and sanitized error.

Do not send secrets. Remove passwords, one-time-code seeds, recovery credentials, private keys, tokens, server passwords, and unredacted vault screenshots.

Common checks

AutoFill candidates can require opening and unlocking passmgr once after installation. Background synchronization timing is controlled by iOS and cannot be guaranteed at an exact minute. A recovery credential is required to decrypt synchronized snapshots after reinstalling.

Response and deletion requests

Support is provided by email without a guaranteed response time. passmgr has no developer-hosted account database; see Data Deletion for device and provider data.
