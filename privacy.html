Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. passmgr is designed as a local-first vault and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

Data stored by the app

Passwords, authenticator secrets, Wi-Fi credentials, settings, recovery material, remote-provider credentials, and passmgr metadata are stored on your devices using app storage and Apple Keychain protections. passmgr does not operate an account service or receive this vault data.

Optional online breach check

If you enable online breached-password checking, passmgr computes a SHA-1 hash on your device and sends only its first five characters to the Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) Pwned Passwords range service. Your complete password, complete hash, username, and service name are never sent. The HTTPS request also exposes ordinary transport information such as the network IP address and a non-user-specific passmgr User-Agent. HIBP states that transient operational and anti-abuse logs may contain submitted request information, headers, and in some cases an IP address. Its privacy policy applies to that processing.

The returned breach count, record revision, and check time are cached only on your device so unchanged passwords do not need repeated requests. You can disable online checks in passmgr settings; disabling the feature stops future HIBP requests and does not send the local cache to passmgr. For conservative App Store disclosure, possible request metadata is classified as linked Other Diagnostic Data and the five-character prefix as unlinked Other Data Types, both used only for App Functionality and never for tracking.

Optional synchronization and backup

When you configure iCloud Keychain, iCloud/Files, WebDAV, SMB/NAS, SFTP, FTP/FTPS, or HTTPS backup, encrypted passmgr data is sent to the destination you select. Provider addresses and credentials are used only to perform your requested connection. The destination operator’s policy also applies.

Permissions

AutoFill, Face ID/Touch ID, notifications, camera/photos, local network, Wi-Fi information, and file access are requested only for related features. You can deny optional permissions, though that feature may not work.

Support and diagnostics

If you email support or explicitly export diagnostics, the information you choose to send is processed to answer your request. Never include passwords, one-time-code seeds, recovery credentials, or provider secrets. Except for the optional HIBP request described above, no diagnostic upload occurs automatically.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete entries, reset local/all data, or uninstall the app, subject to Apple Keychain and iCloud behavior. Remote encrypted backups remain under your chosen provider until removed. See Data Deletion.

Children and changes

passmgr is a general utility and does not knowingly collect children’s personal data. Material policy changes will update this page and its effective date.
